## FAQ Section

**Q: What types of signage do you provide in Dubai?**  
A: We provide shop signs, 3D lettering, flex signboards, reception signs, wall and glass graphics, roll-up displays, vehicle branding, hotel and restaurant signs, exhibition signage and signage maintenance and repair.

**Q: Can you provide both indoor and outdoor signs?**  
A: Yes. We cover both interior and exterior signage applications, including shopfronts, reception signage, glass graphics and hotel and restaurant signboards.

**Q: Do you provide vehicle branding?**  
A: Yes. Vehicle branding is available for business vehicles, with the design and coverage based on the vehicle and branding requirement.

**Q: Can you repair an existing sign?**  
A: Sign maintenance and repair is one of the services we offer. Whether an existing sign can be repaired depends on its condition and which components are damaged.

**Q: Can you work with our existing logo?**  
A: Existing company branding can be used as the basis of the signage artwork. Supplying the clearest available version of your logo helps when preparing the design.

**Q: What information should I send for a quote?**  
A: Send the type of sign required, your location, approximate dimensions if available, photographs of the installation area and your logo or existing artwork.
